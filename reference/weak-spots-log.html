Operation / Formula Errors — 4 questions

Right formula, wrong step — three of these four are the exact same slip: multiplying when the relationship calls for a ratio. Before plugging numbers into any formula question, check whether it's proportional (divide) or compound (multiply) first.

What happenedCh.The fix
Ohm's Law rearrangement9Solving for current is I = V/R, not V = I/R — algebra direction, not a concept miss.
Drawing scale math (1:50)12Multiply the drawn measurement by the scale factor: 20 cm × 50 = 10 m, not 1 m.
Inverse square law16dB = 20 log(D1/D2) — a ratio. 20 log(6×12) multiplies instead of dividing.
Throw distance calculation17Throw distance = throw ratio × image width (2.0 × 4 m = 8 m) — likely divided instead of multiplied.
